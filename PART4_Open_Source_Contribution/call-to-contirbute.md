Over the course of the workshop presentation, the lab, and CTF you have noticed that there are a lot of large rulesets that need our help to fix and uplift the robustness.

In this challenge, I want you to pick a ruleset and find a detection logic bug, verify it and it's fix, raise an issue with the ADE mappings, and then share that with me.

If you don't know where to start, most of these rules in the video and labs aren't patched..

Or, you can go to adeskills and find the list of candidate bypasses that we have identified from mining with adeskills. Adeskills is pretty exhaustive.

[total_candidate_bugs.md](https://github.com/Adversarial-Detection-Engineering/adeskills/blob/main/total_candidate_bugs.md) — 605 candidate bypass findings across Elastic/Sentinel/Crowdstrike/Sigma .
